---
title: Difficulty Levels
description: A guide to the difficulty levels available in REX/M2EX.
---

REX/M2EX add a few new difficulty options.

#### Campaign Difficulty Options
- Fair difficulty sets bonuses for AI and the player to zero, and AI capabilities like hiring mercenaries are enabled, which leads to a level playing field.
- Extreme and Hell are tougher than the highest vanilla difficulty (Very Hard). Don't say we didn't warn you.

[This spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/12dUJozIRBn6Ln6LbyKeoc2vfsgHn4UEMv24WHMt4eJs/edit?pli=1&gid=0#gid=0) details the exact effects of every campaign difficulty level in REX/M2EX.

#### Battle Difficulty Options
The difficulty options are defined in `descr_difficulty` in the `/data` folder. All effects listed there apply to the player.
